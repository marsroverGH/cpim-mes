0045 capabilities:
- Planner/admin close OPEN or PARTIALLY_RECEIVED purchase orders with a required reason; closing is final
- Remaining quantity of a closed order stops counting as MRP, ATP and pegging supply
- Planner/admin cancel OPEN or IN_REWORK supplier NCRs with a required reason; cancelling is final
- Cancelling an NCR leaves the lot quality status unchanged; a PASS inspection releases the lot
- Cancelled NCRs no longer count on the supplier scorecard

0044 capabilities:
- audit_log rejects UPDATE, DELETE and TRUNCATE

0043 capabilities:
- Main quantity columns (inventory ledger, lots, BOM, item safety stock/lot size, MPS, work orders, PO, sales orders) fixed at numeric(20,9)
- Float artefacts stored by v0.42.0 and earlier rounded to 9 decimal places during the upgrade
- Dependent views and column triggers captured, dropped and recreated unchanged
- Ledger reconciliation asserted after rounding; the migration aborts if it does not hold
- Main quantity paths computed in decimal (github.com/shopspring/decimal) in the backend
- scripts/sql/check_quantity_float_artefacts.sql counts stored float artefacts (read-only)

0042 capabilities:
- Separate execution boundary after 0041 side-effect-free Simulation/Publish
- PREPARED -> PREFLIGHT -> APPROVED -> EXECUTING -> terminal lifecycle
- Planner prepare/preflight and Admin-only approval/execution
- Approver/preparer segregation of duties enforced by application and DB constraint
- Immutable published-evidence/action snapshots and canonical hashes
- Wildcard targetRef rejected for execution
- Operational state drift detection immediately before each action
- EXPEDITE_PO via append-only Supplier Scheduling revision evidence
- RESCHEDULE_WO with reschedule signal
- RELEASE_WO via canonical release transaction
- ALTERNATE_WORK_CENTER limited to release-time snapshotted alternatives
- ADD_OVERTIME_CAPACITY via immutable date-scoped capacity override
- Finite CRP and Detailed Scheduling consume executed recovery overtime
- Per-action serializable transaction couples operational mutation and SUCCEEDED evidence
- Durable FAILED/PARTIAL_FAILED/SKIPPED execution evidence
- Terminal execute requests are idempotent and do not replay successful actions
- No automatic generic compensation of previously committed successful actions
